Backup

Your data, saved in your own Google Drive.

Tap Backup Now and choose Google Drive when your phone asks where to save. If you lose or change your phone, install Hira, tap Restore and pick the file from Drive. Everything comes back.

The file is the same kind that Hira for Windows makes, so you can also move a shop between a computer and a phone.

Please back up every week. The data lives on your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is gone unless you have a backup file.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a computer?

No. You can set up your shop, sell, manage stock and take backups using only your phone. If you already use Hira for Windows, you can also move your data between the two with a backup file.

Does it need the internet?

No. Selling, stock and reports work offline. You only need a connection when you save or restore a backup with Google Drive.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, inside the app. It is not sent to us. That is why a weekly Google Drive backup matters: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes the shop data.

How do I get my data back on a new phone?

Install Hira, open Backup, tap Restore and choose your backup file from Google Drive. Your medicines, customers and sales come back.

Is it the same as Hira Live?

No. Hira Live only shows a Windows shop on your phone. Hira Pharmacy for Android is the full app: you can sell and manage stock on it directly.

Is it free? How do I get a license?

The download is free. To use it in your shop, message us on WhatsApp with the Machine ID shown in the app and we will send your license key. Ask us on WhatsApp about phone licensing.

It is a beta. What does that mean?

It is the first release. We tested it on a computer that simulates a phone, but not yet on many real phones or in many shops. Please keep weekly backups and tell us on WhatsApp about anything that looks wrong; we fix quickly.

Why does Android warn me when I install?

The app is shared as a file instead of through the Play Store, so Android asks for your permission once. Only install apps from sources you trust.
