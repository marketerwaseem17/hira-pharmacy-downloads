Release notes

Everything that's changed in Hira Pharmacy.

Every release, in order, with exactly what was added or fixed. Most of it came straight from pharmacies using it day to day.

Version 2.7.3 Latest 18 October 2026
Improved
  • Logo on the WhatsApp bill picture — the bill picture sent on WhatsApp now includes your shop logo.
  • Bill text with the picture — the full bill text (items, total, paid, remaining and khata balance) is sent together with the picture, as its caption or as a message right after it.

Version 2.7.1 18 October 2026
Fixed
  • Manual ledger entries — the Save Entry button in "Add Manual Entry" showed an error in 2.7.0. It now saves, edits and deletes correctly.
  • WhatsApp bill picture — attaching the picture is more reliable. If WhatsApp cannot attach it automatically, the WhatsApp window stays open with the picture copied, so you only press Ctrl+V and Send.
Download this version
Version 2.7.0 18 October 2026
New
  • Send bills on WhatsApp — a new "Send to WhatsApp" button on every invoice. Link WhatsApp once by scanning a QR code, then send the bill straight to the customer's chat as text or as a picture of the receipt. Local numbers (0300...) get +92 added automatically.
  • Khata balance in the message — customers on credit also receive their total balance with the bill.
  • Manual entries in the customer ledger — record cash given, udhaar, a missed bill, or a payment received outside billing. Choose Debit or Credit, set the amount, date and a description. Manual rows are clearly marked and can be edited or deleted.
  • Balances, You Gave / You Got totals, the Dashboard and the Cash Book all update automatically. Untick "show in Cash Book" for an adjustment where no cash moved.
Download this version
Version 2.6.1 17 October 2026
New
  • A cleaner, more professional look — a modern typeface that looks the same on every PC, flat sidebar icons with a clear highlight on the current page, cleaner cards and tables, and refined buttons, input boxes, pop-ups and notifications.
  • Screens now open with a short, smooth fade. Dashboard cards no longer draw a stray flat line when there is no trend yet.
Download this version
Version 2.6.0 17 October 2026
New
  • Pakistani medicine list built in — 10,833 brand names are suggested as you type a medicine name, so you pick instead of typing. Medicines not in your stock show in Sales with a one-click "Add to stock".
  • Sell loose, by the strip or tablet — set tablets per pack (and per strip) on a medicine. The Sales cart gets a Pack / Strip / Tablet choice, prices are worked out from the pack price, and stock shows as "4 packs + 85 tablets".
  • Short List — note medicines customers asked for that you don't have, with quantity, customer, phone and distributor. Print it grouped by distributor, or copy it to WhatsApp. One click adds all your low-stock medicines.
  • Delivery charges — set a default in Settings, switch Delivery on in the cart, and it is added to the total and printed on the receipt and invoice.
  • Default bill discount — set it once in Settings (Rs. or %); every new bill starts with it and it can still be changed on any single bill.
  • Optional GST for tax filers (off by default) — NTN and STRN print on receipts; you choose the rate, whether your prices include GST, and which medicines it applies to. The Sales report shows GST collected.
  • Search Sales History by medicine name, not just invoice number and customer.
  • A faster, cleaner screen — a counter-first Sales screen where Save & Print is always in view, your top sellers one click away, a "Today" band on the dashboard, and slim scrollbars.
  • Fits every screen, from small laptops to large monitors.
Changed
  • Receipt item lines now show only the discount amount in Rs. (no percentage).
