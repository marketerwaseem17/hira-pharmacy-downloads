Release notes

Everything that's changed in Hira Pharmacy.

Every release, in order, with exactly what was added or fixed. Most of it came straight from pharmacies using it day to day.

Version 2.13.0 Latest 26 November 2026
New
  • Free 3-day trial — try Hira before you buy. Download it, install it and press "Start free 3-day trial" on the first screen. No card and no code, internet needed once. The trial is one time per computer.
  • Simpler plans — 1-day trial, 3-day trial, 1 month, 1 year and lifetime. After you pay, press "Check for renewal" inside Hira and the plan continues, with no key to type. A reminder appears in the last week of a plan.
  • Your data stays safe when a plan ends — Hira asks for a renewal, nothing is deleted, and the lock screen has a button to save a backup of your data.
  • Date protection — turning the computer date back no longer extends a trial or a monthly plan. Lifetime licences are not affected.
  • Stock search shows the Pakistan list — search a medicine you do not have and the Stock screen shows it from the Pakistan medicine list, with salt, company, pack and MRP, and an "Add to stock" button that fills the form for you.

Version 2.12.0 21 November 2026
New
  • 21,000 Pakistani medicines built in — type a medicine name and its salt, company, pack size and MRP (from the DRAP price list) appear in the suggestions. Choose one and the Generic Name and the Sale Price fill in by themselves, in Purchase and in Add Medicine. Everything stays editable.
  • Same-salt alternatives — when a customer asks for a medicine you do not have, Sales shows what you do have with the same salt and strength, cheapest first, and one click adds it to the bill.
  • Insights page — what to reorder (with a ready WhatsApp order for each supplier), batches to send back to the supplier before they expire, medicines unsold for 60 days and the money stuck in them, and your fastest earners.
  • Daily summary on WhatsApp — each evening Hira sends you the day's sales, profit, cash in hand, udhaar and stock alerts. Set it up in Settings.
  • Staff and activity log — add your staff in Settings and choose who is working at the counter. Price edits, stock adjustments, deleted purchases, payments and expenses, and sale returns are recorded with the name, in Insights → Activity log.
  • Updates inside the software — Hira checks for a newer version and shows a bar with one-click Update now. Nothing downloads until you click, and your data is kept.
  • Purchase: optional Margin % — the Add Item form has an optional Margin % box with quick 10, 12, 15 and 20%% buttons. Existing medicines bring back their last saved Sale Price. The Sale Price can always be typed by hand.
  • New look — a cleaner sidebar with module search, and a redesigned Hira Live phone app with dark mode.
Fixed
  • Forms no longer close by accident — clicking outside a form (such as New Purchase) used to close it and lose what was typed. Forms now close only with the X, Cancel or Esc.

Version 2.11.0 20 November 2026
New
  • Hira Cloud: your shop live on your phone — see today's sales, profit, cash in hand, stock value, low-stock and near-expiry medicines, the last 20 bills and a 30-day chart on your Android phone with the new Hira Live app. Link the phone with a short code from Settings → Cloud.
  • Automatic encrypted cloud backup — a daily backup is uploaded to the cloud, locked with your own recovery code so nobody else (including us) can read it. If the computer is lost or damaged, restore your whole shop on a new one from Settings → Cloud. The last 7 backups are kept.
  • 1-day free trial — try Hira Cloud for 24 hours without any code, from Settings → Cloud. Hira also tells you about it once after each update. After the trial, message us on WhatsApp to continue monthly.
  • Hira Live for Android — a new app for your phone. Download it from the website. Hira Pharmacy itself still works fully offline.

Version 2.10.0 17 November 2026
New
  • Scan a pack to fill batch and expiry — on Purchase, scan a pack that has a 2D code and the medicine, batch number and expiry fill in by themselves; just type the quantity and press Enter. A code the software has not seen yet is linked to its medicine the first time, from Purchase or from Sales, so nothing has to be typed in beforehand. In Sales, scanning a pack adds that medicine to the bill.
  • Service, hospital and doctor charges — add them to a bill (with the doctor's name if you like) and they show by name on the A4 invoice, the thermal receipt and the WhatsApp bill.
  • Thinner receipt text — choose Thin, Normal or Bold in Settings so numbers stay sharp on any thermal printer. The "Total Savings on this Bill" line is gone, and a thermal receipt now always prints exactly one receipt long.
  • A faster Purchase screen — change quantity and prices right in the list, edit or copy a line, paste rows straight from Excel, or copy a whole earlier purchase as a new one.
  • Default profit margin — set a percentage in Settings and the sale price fills in from the purchase price while you type.

Version 2.9.0 27 October 2026
New
  • Inventory / Audit report — a new tab in Reports that lists every medicine with its stock, batch, expiry and value. Type what you physically count in the Physical Count column and the difference appears by itself, with a count of items that do not match. Print it or save it to Excel for your yearly audit.
  • Short List fills the quantity — "Add low stock" now also writes how much to order (up to the minimum level), so the list is ready to print or send to your distributor. You can still change any quantity.
  • Ctrl+S and Ctrl+P on the sales screen — Ctrl+S saves the sale without printing. Ctrl+P saves and prints at once, or reprints the last sale if the cart is empty. Ctrl+P also prints from the print window.

Version 2.8.0 24 October 2026
New
  • Bill on WhatsApp as plain text — one message with the whole bill: every item, discount, total, paid, remaining and khata balance. Nothing gets cut off, and there is a single Send button.
  • Sends by itself — the app checks that the message really left the chat. If WhatsApp does not accept it, the message stays typed in the WhatsApp window so you only press Enter.

  • Discount shown in Rs. and % on the WhatsApp bill.
  • Easier stock import — Excel import now needs only the medicine name and quantity. Missing prices import as Rs. 0 with a warning, and the sales screen shows "Price not set" for those medicines.

Version 2.6.0 17 October 2026
New
  • Pakistani medicine list built in — 10,833 brand names are suggested as you type a medicine name, so you pick instead of typing. Medicines not in your stock show in Sales with a one-click "Add to stock".
  • Sell loose, by the strip or tablet — set tablets per pack (and per strip) on a medicine. The Sales cart gets a Pack / Strip / Tablet choice, prices are worked out from the pack price, and stock shows as "4 packs + 85 tablets".
  • Short List — note medicines customers asked for that you don't have, with quantity, customer, phone and distributor. Print it grouped by distributor, or copy it to WhatsApp. One click adds all your low-stock medicines.
  • Delivery charges — set a default in Settings, switch Delivery on in the cart, and it is added to the total and printed on the receipt and invoice.
  • Default bill discount — set it once in Settings (Rs. or %); every new bill starts with it and it can still be changed on any single bill.
  • Optional GST for tax filers (off by default) — NTN and STRN print on receipts; you choose the rate, whether your prices include GST, and which medicines it applies to. The Sales report shows GST collected.
  • Search Sales History by medicine name, not just invoice number and customer.
  • A faster, cleaner screen — a counter-first Sales screen where Save & Print is always in view, your top sellers one click away, a "Today" band on the dashboard, and slim scrollbars.
  • Fits every screen, from small laptops to large monitors.
Changed
  • Receipt item lines now show only the discount amount in Rs. (no percentage).
