Release notes

Everything that's changed in Hira Pharmacy.

Every release, in order, with exactly what was added or fixed — most of it came straight from pharmacies actually using it day to day.

Version 2.4.0 Latest 8 October 2026
New
  • Reset All Data in Settings — wipes every medicine, sale, purchase, customer, supplier, expense and cash entry so you can start from zero. Your shop name, logo and license stay activated; a safety backup is saved automatically first, and you must type your pharmacy's exact name to confirm.

Fixed

Version 2.3.0 8 October 2026
New
  • Edit or delete a saved Purchase — stock and cost price are corrected automatically.
  • Quick Add Medicine right from the Sales screen, without navigating away.
  • The Sales cart no longer empties if you step away to another screen and come back.
Fixed
  • Download PDF from the receipt was producing a blank-looking file.
  • Searching a large stock list could take several seconds.
